Privacy Policy — Sterda Official

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sterda Official ("Bot") handles information when you use or add the Bot to a Discord server.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, Sterda Official may process Discord-related information such as User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs, usernames, and server configuration settings.

2. Moderation Data

If moderation features are enabled, the Bot may store information required for moderation features, such as warnings, moderation actions, and related Discord IDs.

3. Ticket and Welcome Systems

If ticket or welcome features are enabled, the Bot may process information necessary to create tickets, manage channels, assign roles, and send welcome messages.

4. Voice System

Temporary voice-channel features may process Discord user, channel, and server identifiers required to create and manage temporary voice channels.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide, operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Bot's features and to respond to support requests.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services only when necessary to operate specific Bot features.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature, security, operational requirements, or legitimate administrative purposes.

8. Data Deletion

Server owners or authorized administrators may request deletion of data associated with their server. Requests may be submitted through the official Sterda Official support/community channels.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Sterda Official. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.
